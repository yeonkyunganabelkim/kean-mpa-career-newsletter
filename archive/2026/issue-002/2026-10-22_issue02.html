KEAN MPA CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Issue #2 · October 22, 2026

Welcome to the second issue of Kean MPA Career Opportunities. This time the list leans toward housing, community and health work, includes two openings in a Jersey City Council office that were sent to me directly, and ends with one look at public service abroad, just for fun.

As before, these are positions I looked through and thought were worth putting in front of you. Please read the qualifications carefully and apply early when something interests you. Several local-government openings come from the NJ League of Municipalities job board, which shows only a short summary, so open the posting for the full requirements and pay.

I have not verified every detail with the employers, and inclusion here is not an endorsement or a guarantee.

Great Opportunities for Current Students & Recent Graduates

Community Outreach Director

Office of Councilwoman Mamta Singh, At-Large, Jersey City Council

Location: Jersey City
Employment type: Full time (an official City of Jersey City position)
Salary: $50,000 annually, full time
Deadline: Not specified

The office's presence in Jersey City's neighborhoods: attending community meetings on the Councilwoman's behalf, tabling at events, running outreach events, and tracking constituent requests from intake to resolution with city departments. Reports to the Chief of Staff.

Why this may fit you: Local government at street level: constituent services, community relationships and working with city departments to get things fixed. Experience in community organizing, constituent services, government, campaigns or nonprofit outreach is preferred. Jersey City residency is preferred, and regular evening and weekend meetings are part of the job.

How to apply: Email a resume and cover letter to stuthomas@jcnj.org with the subject line: APPLICATION Community Outreach Director

Analyst Trainee

NJ Department of the Treasury, Purchase & Property

Location: Ewing, NJ (Distribution & Support Services, Acquisition Unit)
Salary: $53,281.62 – $55,690.52 per year (35-hour week)
Deadline: 5:00 p.m., November 2, 2026
Who may apply: Open to the general public. Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship (student visas such as F-1, J-1, CPT and OPT, and H-1B, are not eligible); New Jersey residency is required within one year of appointment.

Analytical support for the State's purchasing operation: reviewing acquisition data and transactions, preparing statistical and financial reports, and doing market, contract and vendor analyses. After a one-year training period, trainees advance to the full analyst title without a Civil Service exam.

Why this may fit you: A bachelor's degree in any field qualifies (four years of analytical professional experience is the alternative), and the trainee title has a promotion built in, which makes this one of the most direct ways into State government for a new graduate.

Part Time, While You Finish the MPA

A part-time government role you could hold alongside your classes.

Social Media Manager (Part Time)

Office of Councilwoman Mamta Singh, At-Large, Jersey City Council

Location: Jersey City
Employment type: Part time (an official City of Jersey City position)
Salary: $15,000 annually, part time
Deadline: Not specified

Films and edits short videos of the Councilwoman's work, manages her social media accounts, and keeps residents informed about the office and how to get involved. Hours are built around events and set with the Chief of Staff.

Why this may fit you: If you are still in the program and want government experience, this is a way into a Council office while you study: public communication for an elected official. Jersey City residency is preferred; evening and weekend events.

How to apply: Email a resume and cover letter to stuthomas@jcnj.org with the subject line: APPLICATION Social Media Manager

🌍 JUST FOR FUN: PUBLIC SERVICE AROUND THE WORLD

Ever wonder what public-service careers look like outside New Jersey? Here's one opportunity that caught my eye this week. Its citizenship, work-authorization and security-clearance rules put it out of reach for most of us — this section is mostly here to help us look beyond our usual map and see where public-administration skills show up around the world.

Graduate Policy Programme (Policy Adviser)

HM Treasury, United Kingdom

Location: London or Darlington
Deadline: 12 noon (UK time), November 5, 2026
Who may apply: British citizens and EEA nationals with the right to work in the UK without sponsorship. Security clearance is required, normally with three of the last five years lived in the UK.

Why this is fun to look at: A finance ministry hires graduates of any subject straight into policy-adviser work, and lets them choose between London and Darlington.
