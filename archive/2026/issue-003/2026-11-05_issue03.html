KEAN MPA CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Issue #3 · November 5, 2026

Welcome to the third issue of Kean MPA Career Opportunities. This time the list leans toward budgets, evaluation and research, with more State and county government than before, two foundations, and one look at public service abroad, just for fun.

Several of this issue's government jobs are New Jersey Civil Service announcements. Each one limits who may apply by where you live, so I have put that right on the card: please check it before anything else. You apply through the Civil Service Commission's website by the closing date, and some titles involve an exam.

As always, read the qualifications carefully and apply early when something interests you.

I have not verified every detail with the employers, and inclusion here is not an endorsement or a guarantee.

Location: The current vacancy is in Atlantic County; the list from this announcement will be used for future vacancies in all counties
Employment type: Full time, 35 hours per week
Salary: $58,222.22 – $84,763.22 per year
Deadline: November 23, 2026

Auditor Taxation Trainee

NJ Department of the Treasury, Division of Taxation

Location: Office Audit in Trenton; Field Audit offices in Trenton, Hackensack, Cranford, Freehold, Galloway and Camden
Employment type: Full time, 35 hours per week
Salary: $63,662.98 – $66,592.01 (Office Audit); $66,592.01 – $69,668.20 (Field Audit)
Deadline: 5:00 p.m., December 31, 2026 (interviews in late January 2027)
Who may apply: Open to the general public. Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship (student visas such as F-1, J-1, CPT and OPT, and H-1B, are not eligible); New Jersey residency is required within one year of appointment.

Examining tax returns, financial statements and records for the Division of Taxation, either in the office or in the field at taxpayers' locations. After a one-year training period, trainees advance to the full auditor title without a Civil Service exam. December 2026 graduates are encouraged to apply.

Why this may fit you: A State job with a promotion built in and a long runway to apply. Check your coursework first: it asks for a bachelor's degree that includes, or is supplemented by, 21 credits in accounting (government or municipal accounting courses can count), or a CPA. A New Jersey driver's license is required.

If Something Catches Your Eye, Come Talk to Us

If a position catches your eye, send it our way or come talk to us. We know many of you beyond what shows up on a résumé—your work, your interests, your strengths, and the direction you may be trying to move in. We can help you think through whether a role makes sense, how to frame your experience, or how to prepare for the application or interview. You do not need to figure out every next step by yourself. We're happy to be part of the process.

Job postings can change or close quickly. Please confirm requirements and deadlines on the employer's original posting, or with the office named in the application instructions, before applying.

And tell me what you would like to see more of in future issues—local government, nonprofits, higher education, healthcare, policy, data, or something else.

If one of these positions makes you think, "Should I go for this?" send it to me. We can talk it through.
