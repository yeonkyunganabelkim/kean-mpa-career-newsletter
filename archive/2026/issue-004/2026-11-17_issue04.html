Special Opportunities for Students

Japan 2027: Global Research & Field Experience

Kean University, Center for Undergraduate Research and Fellowships (CURF)

Dates: May 21–30, 2027 (10 days)
Route: Kobe → Sendai/Tohoku → Tokyo
Focus: Disaster resilience, public policy and community recovery
Deadline: Thursday, December 17, 2026
Who may apply: Graduate students, and undergraduate juniors or seniors with faculty advisor approval. Limited spots available.
Contact: Kean Center for Undergraduate Research and Fellowships (CURF), curf@kean.edu

A ten-day field experience in three cities, with three perspectives on how communities recover: Kobe (rebuilding after disaster: lessons from the 1995 earthquake), Sendai and the Tohoku region (resilience in action: recovery and community strength after the 2011 earthquake and tsunami) and Tokyo (policy, innovation and governance: national strategies, technology and urban resilience). Participants see disaster recovery in action, explore how different sectors work together, build research skills through site visits, interviews and comparative studies, engage with Japanese universities, experts and community organizations, and learn about volunteerism, nonprofits and public-private solutions.

The flyer invites students interested in careers in public administration and public policy; emergency and disaster management; international relations and development; nonprofit management and community leadership; urban planning and sustainability; and social research and public service. Selection is based on academic performance (GPA), career interests, a statement of interest, and research experience or potential.

To apply, scan the QR code on the flyer, or write to CURF at curf@kean.edu with questions.

Why this may fit you: This one is open to you as a graduate student, and its themes read like an MPA syllabus: emergency management, public policy, nonprofits and community recovery, studied on the ground in places that have rebuilt after disaster. If you have been looking for research or international experience to bring to your next job, start here.

2027 Founders' Fellows Program

American Society for Public Administration (ASPA)

Class: 2027–2028 Founders' Fellows (about 30 Fellows are selected)
Deadline: Tuesday, December 15, 2026, 11:59 p.m. Eastern time
Who may apply: Currently enrolled graduate students (master's or doctoral level), or new professionals within three years of graduating from an academic program.
ASPA membership: Required. You must be a current ASPA member to apply, and the person who writes your letter of recommendation must also be a current ASPA member.
What you submit: Contact and biographical information, an outline of your career and research interests, a resume or CV, an essay, and one letter of recommendation (uploaded with the application, not sent separately or by email)

ASPA, the national professional association for public administration, selects a class of Founders' Fellows each year from graduate students and new professionals. Fellows are guaranteed the opportunity to present their work at ASPA's 2028 Annual Conference, with complimentary conference registration and a hotel room. Each Fellow also has a year-long mentorship with a senior mentor in the field and professional development webinars designed for the class.

Why this may fit you: This one is made for MPA students. A guaranteed national conference presentation, a senior mentor for a full year and a network of peers across the country are hard to find at this stage of a career. Start early: check your ASPA membership, ask your recommender now, and make sure they are a current member too.

United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Spain

Location: Madrid, Spain
Type: Internship, full time, 6 months
Apply before: 11:59 p.m. Eastern time, Monday, November 30, 2026
Who may apply: Final-year undergraduates, current graduate students, or recent graduates who would start the internship within one year of graduating. Why this is worth a look: A United Nations internship open to graduate students, where public finance, conservation and communications meet. Before you apply, weigh the stipend against the cost of living in Madrid.

If Something Catches Your Eye, Come Talk to Us

If a position catches your eye, send it our way or come talk to us. We know many of you beyond what shows up on a résumé—your work, your interests, your strengths, and the direction you may be trying to move in. We can help you think through whether a role makes sense, how to frame your experience, or how to prepare for the application or interview.
