# KEAN MPA CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

**Issue #1 · October 2026**

Welcome to the first issue of Kean MPA Career Opportunities. Every two weeks we will share a short, selected list of openings in New Jersey government, nonprofits and higher education that may be relevant to MPA students, recent graduates and alumni.

These are postings we came across and thought worth your attention. We have not verified every detail with the employers, and inclusion here is not an endorsement. Please read the qualifications carefully, and if a position interests you, apply early.

## You Don't Have to Apply Alone

Whatever position you are considering, we hope you will talk with us before or while you apply. Your MPA faculty know your academic work, your strengths and what you are aiming for, and we can help you think through whether a job is a good fit, how to present your experience, and how to prepare for the application and the interview. If you would like a second pair of eyes on an opportunity, a resume, a cover letter or your overall approach, reach out to any MPA faculty member. We are glad to help.

---

Job postings can change or close quickly. Please confirm requirements and deadlines on the employer's original posting before applying.

If there are sectors or types of positions you would like us to feature in future issues, let us know.

We are glad to see you taking the next step, and we are here when you need us.

— The Kean MPA Program
